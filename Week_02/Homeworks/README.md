# Week 02 — Homework

This directory contains the homework problems corresponding to **Week 02** of the Computational Astrophysics course.

The homework focuses on the numerical differentiation techniques introduced during the week, particularly the use of finite differences to approximate derivatives and the analysis of their numerical errors.

The problems may involve the implementation and comparison of forward, backward, and centered finite difference methods, as well as the study of their accuracy and dependence on the step size.
